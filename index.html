About the property

Description property

1 East View, East Hill, Cobh, Co. Cork, P24 VY64

DNG Spillane are delighted to present 1 East View, East Hill, Cobh to the open market, an attractive three-bedroom mid-terrace residence occupying an enviable elevated position and enjoying truly spectacular, uninterrupted views across Cork Harbour.

Extending to approximately 82 sq. metres, this well-proportioned home is presented in good condition throughout and combines comfortable accommodation with one of its most impressive attributes – its exceptional harbour setting. From its elevated position on East Hill, the property commands a wonderful outlook across Cork Harbour, with the ever-changing maritime landscape providing an impressive backdrop to everyday living.

The accommodation is arranged over two floors and has a practical, traditional layout. An entrance porch leads into the kitchen/dining area, which provides a generous everyday living and dining space. The ground floor is completed by a separate living room, while upstairs there are three bedrooms together with the main family bathroom. The front-facing rooms take full advantage of the property's elevated position, enjoying excellent views across the harbour and surrounding coastline.

Externally, the private decking area to the front is a particularly appealing feature of the property. Positioned to maximise the panoramic outlook, it provides an ideal vantage point from which to enjoy the magnificent harbour views and watch the constant activity of Cork Harbour below. A small private yard is situated to the rear of the property.

The residence benefits from oil-fired central heating and is presented in good overall condition, offering a home that can be comfortably occupied while still providing scope for a new owner to personalise and enhance the property to suit their own requirements over time.

Location is another significant advantage. East Hill is an established and highly convenient residential setting within easy walking distance of Cobh town centre, where an excellent selection of shops, cafés, restaurants, schools and everyday services are available. Cobh Railway Station is also within walking distance, providing regular rail connections to Cork City and making the property particularly convenient for commuters.

Cobh itself remains one of East Cork's most distinctive coastal towns, renowned for its colourful streetscape, historic waterfront and spectacular natural harbour. No. 1 East View is ideally positioned to enjoy all that the town has to offer while retaining an elevated residential setting and a remarkable outlook across the water.

With its three-bedroom accommodation, private front decking, convenient location and, above all, its exceptional unrestricted Cork Harbour views, 1 East View represents an excellent opportunity to acquire a home in a particularly attractive part of Cobh.

Viewing is highly recommended through sole selling agents DNG Spillane.
